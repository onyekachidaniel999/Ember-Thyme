Our Story

Where Every Flame
Tells a Story

Born from a love of fire, craft, and the belief that every great meal deserves to be remembered.

10+ Years of Excellence

A Kitchen Built on
Passion & Fire

Ember & Thyme was born in 2014 from a simple but uncompromising idea — that great food should tell a story. Founded by Chef Marco Rossi after a decade working in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe, our restaurant brought something Toronto had never quite experienced before: a wood-fired kitchen where every dish is shaped by flame.

We chose King Street West not by accident but by instinct. This neighbourhood has always attracted people who refuse to settle — artists, entrepreneurs, dreamers. It felt like the right place to build something that would last.

Over ten years later, we've served over 200,000 guests, earned a reputation as one of Canada's most distinctive dining destinations, and never once compromised on the things that matter most: ingredients, technique, and the warmth of genuine hospitality.

What We Stand For

Our Philosophy

🔥
Fire First
Wood fire is not a technique — it's a philosophy. Every dish that leaves our kitchen has been shaped by real flame, real smoke, and real heat. We believe fire reveals the truth in every ingredient.
🌿
Local & Seasonal
We source from Ontario farms we trust by name. Our menu changes with the seasons — not as a trend, but because food tastes better when it's fresh, close, and grown with care.
🍷
Crafted with Intention
Nothing on our menu is accidental. Every pairing, every garnish, every plating decision is deliberate. We believe the details are what separate a good meal from an unforgettable one.
🤝
Genuine Hospitality
Fine dining shouldn't feel stiff. We want you to feel like a valued guest in our home — relaxed, cared for, and certain that every person in this building is here to make your evening exceptional.
♻️
Sustainable Kitchen
We compost, we reduce waste, and we work with suppliers who share our values. A restaurant that cares about its community should also care about the planet that feeds it.
⭐
No Compromise
We've never used a shortcut that compromised the food. Our pasta is made by hand every morning. Our bread is baked fresh daily. Some things are worth the extra effort — everything we serve is one of them.

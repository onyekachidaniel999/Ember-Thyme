Our Story

Fired by Passion,
Seasoned by Time

Ember & Thyme was born from a simple belief — that great food is not just eaten, it's felt. Founded in 2014 in Toronto, we combine the ancient art of open-flame cooking with modern culinary innovation to create dishes that linger long after the last bite.

Every ingredient is sourced fresh. Every dish is crafted with intention. Every visit is designed to be unforgettable.

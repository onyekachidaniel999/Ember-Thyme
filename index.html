Our Story

Where Every Flame
Tells a Story

Born from a love of fire, craft, and the belief that every great meal deserves to be remembered.

10+ Years of Excellence

A Kitchen Built on
Passion & Fire

Ember & Thyme was born in 2014 from a simple but uncompromising idea — that great food should tell a story. Founded by Chef Marco Rossi after a decade working in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe, our restaurant brought something Toronto had never quite experienced before: a wood-fired kitchen where every dish is shaped by flame.

We chose King Street West not by accident but by instinct. This neighbourhood has always attracted people who refuse to settle — artists, entrepreneurs, dreamers. It felt like the right place to build something that would last.

Over ten years later, we've served over 200,000 guests, earned a reputation as one of Canada's most distinctive dining destinations, and never once compromised on the things that matter most: ingredients, technique, and the warmth of genuine hospitality.

Marco Rossi
Founder & Head Chef

What We Stand For

Our Philosophy

🔥
Fire First
Wood fire is not a technique — it's a philosophy. Every dish that leaves our kitchen has been shaped by real flame, real smoke, and real heat.
🌿
Local & Seasonal
We source from Ontario farms we trust by name. Our menu changes with the seasons — because food tastes better when it's fresh and close.
🍷
Crafted with Intention
Nothing on our menu is accidental. Every pairing, every garnish, every plating decision is deliberate and purposeful.
🤝
Genuine Hospitality
Fine dining shouldn't feel stiff. We want you to feel like a valued guest in our home — relaxed, cared for, and celebrated.
♻️
Sustainable Kitchen
We compost, we reduce waste, and we work with suppliers who share our values. A great restaurant should care about the planet that feeds it.
⭐
No Compromise
Our pasta is made by hand every morning. Our bread is baked fresh daily. Some things are worth the extra effort — everything we serve is one of them.

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a reservation?
Walk-ins are welcome but we strongly recommend reserving, especially on weekends. Parties of 6+ require advance booking.
Is there a dress code?
We ask for smart casual. Feel comfortable, but dressed for the occasion — think dinner-ready, not beach-ready.
Do you cater to dietary requirements?
Absolutely. Please mention any allergies or dietary needs when booking. Our kitchen can accommodate most requirements with advance notice.
Can I host a private event?
Yes! We have a private dining room that seats up to 20 guests. Email hello@emberthyme.ca to discuss availability.
What's your cancellation policy?
We ask for at least 24 hours notice. Late cancellations for large parties may be subject to a fee.
Is there parking nearby?
Street parking is available on King Street West after 6pm. Paid lot on Adelaide Street, one block north.
